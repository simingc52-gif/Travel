Chinese Customs(The day near to the Customs is use the traditional Chinese lunar calendar)

春节(Spring Festival)(first day of the first month)

Pasting couplets and staying up on New Year's Eve; exchanging greetings and welcoming the new year with family.

端午(Dragon Boat Festival)(fifth day of the fifth month)

Eating *zongzi* and racing dragon boats; hanging mugwort to ward off evil and seek blessings.

中秋(Mid-Autumn Festival)(15th day of the 8th month)

Admiring the moon and sharing mooncakes; praying for family reunion.

冬至(Winter Solstice)

Eating dumplings in the North and glutinous rice balls in the South for warmth.

重阳(Double Ninth Festival)(ninth day of the ninth month)

Climbing heights and admiring chrysanthemums; honoring the elderly and wishing for their well-being.
