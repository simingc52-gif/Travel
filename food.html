A Bite of China

四川火锅(Sichuan hot pot)

Spicy, numbing, and savory, with bubbling red chili oil—tripe and duck intestine are the soul of the dish, embodying the vibrant, bustling spirit of Bashu cuisine.

广式早茶(Cantonese Dim Sum)

Shrimp dumplings, siu mai, and rice noodle rolls—light, fresh, and sweet—embodying the flavors of Lingnan’s unhurried lifestyle.

西湖醋鱼(West Lake Fish With Vinegar Sauce)

A classic Hangzhou dish featuring a delightful balance of sweet and sour flavors—an exquisite taste characteristic of the Jiangnan water towns.

北京烤鸭(Beijing Roast Duck)

Crispy skin and tender meat—a world-renowned signature delicacy of Beijing, enjoyed by wrapping the meat in pancakes and dipping it in sauce.

佛跳墙(Buddha Jumps Over the Wall)

The broth is rich and full-bodied, with a silky-smooth texture; the flavor is savory yet delicate.

东坡肉(Dongpo Pork)

Slow-cooked until tender and succulent; rich and savory with a melt-in-the-mouth texture, yet not greasy.

水煮鱼(Poached Fish in Chili Oil)

Numbing, spicy, and aromatic; the fish fillets are silky-smooth and flavorful, bathed in a rich red chili oil that delivers a thrilling taste experience.

清蒸鲈鱼(Steamed Sea Bass)

Fresh, light, and delicate; the meat is tender, preserving the ingredient's natural flavor with a perfect balance of savoriness.

红烧肉(Braised Pork Belly)

Glossy red in color with melt-in-the-mouth tenderness; a harmonious blend of sweet and savory flavors that is rich yet not greasy.

辣子鸡(Spicy Diced Chicken)

Dry-fried to aromatic perfection with a numbing, spicy kick; the chicken is firm yet tender, offering an addictive heat and a lingering, savory finish.

红烧狮子头(Braised Lion's Head Meatballs)

Tender and juicy meatballs in a rich, flavorful broth; soft, savory, and aromatic.

糖醋排骨(Sweet and Sour Spare Ribs)

Appetizingly tangy and sweet; the meat is firm, the glaze is glossy, and the flavor is savory and delicious.

葱烧海参(Braised Sea Cucumber with Scallions)

Rich scallion aroma; the sea cucumber is firm yet springy and tender, offering a savory, mellow depth and a sophisticated mouthfeel.
