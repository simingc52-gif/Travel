A magnificent land of picturesque scenery.

万里长城(Great Wall)

A wonder of the world, winding through the mountains and bearing witness to a thousand years of history's vicissitudes.

桂林山水(The Landscapes of Guilin)

A landscape of clear waters and lush hills, with an array of extraordinary peaks—karst scenery that is unsurpassed in the world.

黄山云海(Sea of ​​Clouds at Huangshan)

With uniquely shaped pines and fantastic rock formations shrouded in mist and clouds, the mountain scenery resembles a traditional ink-and-wash painting.

张家界(Zhangjiajie)

A forest of quartz sandstone peaks, a realm of floating mountains, and a fantastical geological landscape.

三亚亚龙湾(Yalong Bay, Sanya)

Azure waters and silvery sands, the gentle rustle of coconut palms, and the setting sun spreading across the sea's surface.

九寨沟(Jiuzhaigou)

Crystal-clear waters, forests of vibrant hues and layered greenery, and alpine lakes resembling scattered emeralds.
